1. Introduction

Welcome to Business Manager, a business management and point-of-sale (POS) application developed by Saim. The application helps users organize products, inventory, customers, suppliers, sales, purchases, expenses, invoices, and business reports.

This policy applies to the Business Manager application and explains our approach to information privacy, storage, security, and user control.

By using the application, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please discontinue using the application.

2. Information We Handle

Information entered into Business Manager depends on how you use its features. The application may store the following information locally on your device:

Business and Owner Information

  • Business name and business contact details.
  • Owner information entered in settings.
  • Business address and invoice details, if provided.
  • Currency, language, and application preferences.

Products and Inventory

  • Product names, categories, prices, and descriptions.
  • Stock quantities and low-stock information.
  • Product images or related details, if supported and used.

Customers and Suppliers

  • Names, phone numbers, addresses, and other details you enter.
  • Customer transaction histories and supplier records.
  • Purchase records and related payment information.

Sales, Expenses, and Invoices

  • Sales transactions, sale items, quantities, and prices.
  • Payments, outstanding balances, and expense records.
  • Invoice numbers, totals, dates, and generated documents.
  • Purchase history and business performance records.
Important: Avoid entering passwords, bank card security codes, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information unless a feature specifically requires it and you have a lawful reason to record it.

We do not intentionally collect information simply because it exists on your device. Information handling depends on the app's implemented functionality and the actions you take.

3. Local Data Storage

Business Manager is designed to use a local SQLite database through Flutter's Sqflite functionality for storing business records.

Where the relevant features are implemented, your information is stored locally on the device rather than automatically uploaded to a server operated by the developer.

  • Business records can be available without an internet connection when their features support offline use.
  • The developer does not automatically receive locally stored records merely because you use the application.
  • Device backups, exported files, shared invoices, or third-party services may handle copies of your information separately.

Local storage does not guarantee that information is encrypted. The level of protection depends on the device, operating system, app implementation, and any additional security measures enabled.

4. Device Permissions

Business Manager may request access to certain device features when you use corresponding functionality. Permission availability depends on your device and the installed version of the application.

Camera and Barcode Scanning

If barcode scanning is implemented, camera access may be used to scan product barcodes or QR codes. Camera access should be used for the feature you initiate.

Photos and Images

If image selection is supported, photo or image access may be used to select product images or other content you choose to add.

Files and Document Access

File access may be required to export or import database backups, save PDF invoices, or select documents for restoration.

Printing and Sharing

Printing and sharing features may allow you to send invoices or exported files to a printer, another application, or a recipient you choose.

You can manage available permissions through your device settings. Revoking a permission may prevent the associated feature from working correctly.

Business Manager does not need access to a permission merely because that permission is listed here. Actual access depends on the app's implementation and the feature being used.

5. How Information Is Used

Information entered into the application is used to provide its business management functionality, including:

  • Managing products, categories, and inventory.
  • Recording sales, purchases, and expenses.
  • Maintaining customer and supplier records.
  • Calculating totals, balances, and business summaries.
  • Generating invoices and PDF documents.
  • Preparing reports and analytics from business records.
  • Saving preferences such as currency and theme.
  • Creating and restoring user-initiated backups.

These operations are intended to help you manage your own business. The developer does not claim ownership of the business records you enter.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not intentionally transmit your locally stored business database to the developer or sell your business records to third parties as part of the app's intended local-storage functionality.

Information may leave your device when you choose to share or export a file, use a third-party application, or enable an integration that transmits information.

If you share an invoice or backup through email, messaging, cloud storage, or another application, that service may process the information according to its own privacy policy.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or valid legal process, if such information is within our possession or control.

We cannot control how recipients handle files after you share them.

7. Database Backups and Exports

Business Manager may provide functionality to export a database backup and restore data from a previously exported file.

Backup files may contain sensitive business information, including customer details, supplier records, sales, expenses, and financial balances.

  • Keep backup files in a secure location.
  • Share backups only with people you trust and who are authorized to access the data.
  • Avoid storing unprotected backups in public or shared folders.
  • Verify the source of a backup before restoring it.
  • Keep a separate backup before restoring a file that could replace existing information.

Exported backups may remain available outside the application even after records are deleted from the app. You are responsible for managing copies stored in locations you control.

8. Data Security

We recognize that business records can be sensitive. The application is designed to keep its primary business records in local storage, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

To help protect your information, we recommend:

  • Using a strong device screen lock.
  • Keeping your operating system and app updated.
  • Restricting access to your device.
  • Protecting exported files and database backups.
  • Avoiding the sharing of sensitive records through untrusted services.

We cannot guarantee absolute security against unauthorized access, malware, device loss, hardware failure, or accidental deletion.

Unless specifically implemented and documented in the application, do not assume that the SQLite database or exported backup files are encrypted or password-protected.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored records generally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the relevant application data, uninstall the app, or the information is removed through an implemented application feature.

Uninstalling the application may remove its local database and related private files, depending on the operating system and storage location. It may not remove files you exported or copies held by other applications or backup services.

Before uninstalling the application or clearing its data, export a backup if you want to preserve your business records.

We do not have a remote copy of locally stored information to delete unless that information has separately been transmitted to a service under your direction.

10. Your Choices and Control

You maintain control over the business information you choose to enter and how you use the available application features.

  • Access: View records using the application's available screens.
  • Correction: Edit records where the application provides an edit feature.
  • Deletion: Delete records using available deletion features.
  • Export: Use available backup and export features to preserve data.
  • Permissions: Manage camera, photo, and file permissions through device settings.

Available controls depend on the version of the application you use. You may contact the developer with privacy questions using the contact details below.
